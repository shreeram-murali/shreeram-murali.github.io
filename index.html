Home

I’m a PhD student in the Cyber-physical Systems Group at Aalto University, Finland, supervised by Prof. Dominik Baumann and Prof. Shankar Deka. I’m also affiliated with the Finnish Center for Artificial Intelligence.

My research sits at the intersection of machine learning, computational statistics, and autonomous control. I’m interested in making learning-based methods safer, computationally lightweight, and more robust for applications in robotics and healthcare.

Outside of this, I’m into photography, filmmaking, film-watching, and noodling on my acoustic guitar.
